# Product Context

## Business Problem
Precision titanium CNC machining buyers (aerospace, medical, energy) need a technical, trustworthy digital presence that demonstrates manufacturing capability, quality certifications (AS9100D, ISO 9001:2015), and engineering expertise across 12 global markets.

## User Personas
1. **Procurement Engineer** — needs material specs, tolerance data, certifications
2. **Design Engineer** — needs DFM feedback, CAD upload, technical guidelines
3. **Business Owner** — needs trust signals, facility tour, case studies

## Key Differentiators
- 120+ advanced CNC machining centers
- AS9100D & ISO 9001:2015 certified
- < 48h technical DFM & RFQ response
- 5,000+ pcs monthly titanium output
- 15+ facilities globally

## Content Strategy
- Service pages with detailed process breakdowns
- Equipment knowledge graph (13+ machine types)
- Material grades library (titanium alloys)
- Industry application pages (8 industries)
- Engineering tools (tolerance checker, grade finder, weight calculator)
- Blog with technical articles
