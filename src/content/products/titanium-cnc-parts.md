BOZE CNC Ti offers world-class precision CNC machining services for titanium and other exotic metal components. With over **50 years of combined experience** in the titanium manufacturing industry, we deliver components that meet the most demanding specifications across aerospace, medical, automotive, and industrial applications.

## Our CNC Machining Capabilities

We operate a modern fleet of **3-axis, 4-axis, and 5-axis CNC machining centers** equipped with advanced control systems and real-time monitoring. This enables us to achieve complex geometries with exceptional precision and repeatability.

### Key Machining Processes

- **High-Speed Milling** — Achieve rapid material removal rates while maintaining tight tolerances of ±0.005mm. Ideal for complex 3D contours and thin-wall structures.
- **Precision Turning** — CNC lathes with live tooling for multi-diameter shafts, threaded components, and rotationally symmetrical parts.
- **Multi-Axis Machining** — 5-axis simultaneous machining reduces setups, improves accuracy, and enables complex undercut geometries.
- **EDM (Electrical Discharge Machining)** — Wire EDM and sinker EDM for tight internal features, sharp corners, and hardened materials.
- **Grinding & Finishing** — Surface grinding, cylindrical grinding, and centerless grinding to achieve surface finishes down to Ra 0.4 µm.
