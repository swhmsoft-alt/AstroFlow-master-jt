## Material overview

**Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5)** is the workhorse alpha-beta titanium alloy, accounting for over 50% of all titanium usage globally. The "ELI" variant — **Grade 23 (Extra Low Interstitial)** — tightens the upper limits on interstitial elements (O, N, C, Fe) to deliver measurably better fracture toughness, fatigue life, and ductility.

## When to choose Grade 5

- Aerospace structural components where AMS 4928 is the controlling spec.
- High-stress rotating parts and engine brackets (450°C upper service limit).
- Cost-sensitive programs where the tighter ELI chemistry is not mandated.
- Marine and chemical process equipment (excellent corrosion resistance, ASTM B348 stock form).

## When to choose Grade 23

- Long-term implantable medical devices (ASTM F136, ISO 5832-3).
- Cryogenic and deep-subsea housings (superior fracture toughness at low temperatures).
- High-cycle fatigue applications: turbine blades, downhole tools, racing connecting rods.
- Any procurement where AS9100D traceability and reduced inclusion content is mandated.

## Procurement cost drivers

Grade 23 typically carries a **15-30% raw-material premium** over Grade 5 due to tighter melt controls, additional VAR (Vacuum Arc Remelting) cycles, and lower allowable batch yield. For high-mix B2B programs, partnering with a supplier that holds both grades in bar, plate, and billet form (with EN 10204 3.1 mill cert traceability) eliminates the 6-12 week mill lead time penalty.
