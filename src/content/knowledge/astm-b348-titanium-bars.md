## What ASTM B348 covers

ASTM B348 is the foundational specification for **titanium and titanium alloy bars and billets**. It defines chemical composition limits, tensile/yield/elongation mechanical requirements, and permissible variation in dimensions and straightness. The spec covers 30+ grades — Grades 1-4 commercially pure, Grades 5/5ELI/9/23 alpha-beta, Grades 19/20/21 beta alloys.

## Reading the mechanical table

The mechanical properties table in B348 separates requirements by **product form** (bar vs billet) and **condition** (annealed, solution-treated, solution-treated + aged). For Grade 5 bar in annealed condition, the spec requires:

- Tensile strength ≥ 895 MPa (130 ksi)
- Yield strength ≥ 828 MPa (120 ksi)
- Elongation ≥ 10% in 4D
- Reduction of area ≥ 25%

If a mill cert shows values at the *lower* end of the envelope, the bar is technically compliant but will be harder to machine without accelerated tool wear. Always request the actual test report (not just the heat number) for high-precision programs.

## Common procurement pitfalls

- **Heat lot vs piece cert** — request a per-piece mechanical test report for medical and aerospace.
- **Surface condition** — B348 allows hot-rolled, cold-finished, or rough-turned. Specify explicitly.
- **DFARS compliance** — for US DoD end-use, ensure the mill cert explicitly states DFARS 252.225-7014 compliance.
